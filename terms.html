1. Doorstep Inspection Fee & Diagnostic Adjustment

A standard doorstep inspection and diagnosis charge of ₹350 INR applies to all physical technician visits within Kadapa city and Bangalore service boundaries.

✓ 100% Fee Adjustment Guarantee:

If you proceed with the suggested repair or component replacement, the entire ₹350 inspection fee is completely deducted from your final repair invoice. You only pay for parts and repair labor.

2. Two-Step Doorstep Pricing & Quotation Approval

  • Online prices shown on the website and mobile apps are baseline estimates for standard labor tasks.
  • Upon physical doorstep inspection, the technician will examine internal parts (compressor, run capacitor, condenser coils, refrigerant pressure) and present an itemized quotation.
  • Work commences only after verbal or digital quotation approval by the customer. No hidden surcharges are levied.

3. 30-Day Workmanship Guarantee & Genuine Spares

All repairs executed by Master Shaik Sameer and certified partner technicians include our official 30-Day Doorstep Guarantee:

  • If the identical technical fault reoccurs within 30 days of service completion, a technician visits at ₹0 visiting charge to inspect and correct the issue.
  • All refrigerant refills (R-32, R-410A, R-134a) and electrical capacitors installed are sourced from verified brand distributors. Manufacturer warranties on specific compressor parts apply per brand terms.

4. Payment Modes & Zero Surcharge

Customers pay ₹0 in advance. Payment is collected strictly after repair completion and testing via:

  • Direct NPCI Dynamic UPI QR: Scanned on-site using Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm with 0% gateway commission.
  • Cash on Delivery (Doorstep Cash): Paid directly to the technician against a digital receipt.

5. Service Cancellation

Doorstep bookings may be rescheduled or cancelled at ₹0 penalty prior to technician arrival. If the technician has already completed travel and arrived at the customer doorstep, the standard ₹350 diagnostic visit fee applies if repair is cancelled by the customer.
